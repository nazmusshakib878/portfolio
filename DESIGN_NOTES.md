# Design notes

The local reference established a dark cinematic stage, editorial typography, restrained violet/teal light and delayed mouse response. The original implementation attempted a procedural character, but visual review showed that its face proportions and styling were not production quality.

The final direction deliberately uses Md. Nazmus Shakib's real portrait. Motion springs provide only a few pixels of pointer parallax while the portrait remains recognizable and professional. A soft violet field, thin frame and restrained teal/violet edge lighting create depth without fabricating identity.

Mobile and reduced-motion users receive a stable portrait with no tracking. Project visuals are original abstract covers and are not presented as real screenshots.
